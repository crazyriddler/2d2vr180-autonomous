2D2VR180 splat viewer
Mouse: drag = orbit · right-drag = pan · wheel = zoom · arrow keys = pan · R = reset view.
VR: connect your headset to this PC (Quest Link / Air Link / Virtual Desktop / SteamVR), then press ENTER VR at the bottom of the page. You start at eye level where the original camera stood, with the horizon levelled.
Controllers: left stick = move · right stick left/right = turn 30° · right stick up/down = rise/sink · A or X = back to the start.
Only what the camera saw exists in 3D — you will see empty space behind objects and outside the photo.
