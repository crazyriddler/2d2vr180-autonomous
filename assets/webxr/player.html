2D2VR player
Mouse: drag = orbit · right-drag = pan · wheel = zoom · R = reset view.
Space = play / pause · , and . = previous / next frame.
 + and − = make everything bigger / smaller (if people do not look life-size) · 0 = original size.
VR: connect your headset to this PC (Quest Link / Air Link / Virtual Desktop / SteamVR), then press ENTER VR. You start where the camera stood, with the horizon levelled.
Controllers: A or X (hold) = smaller · B or Y (hold) = bigger · left stick = move · right stick left/right = turn 30° · right stick up/down = rise/sink · stick click = back to the start and the original size · trigger = play / pause.
Only what the camera saw exists in 3D.
