2D2VR180 4D player
Mouse: drag = orbit · right-drag = pan · wheel = zoom · R = reset view.
Space = play / pause · , and . = previous / next frame · F = follow the filming camera.
VR: connect your headset to this PC (Quest Link / Air Link / Virtual Desktop / SteamVR), then press ENTER VR. You start where the camera stood, with the horizon levelled.
Controllers: left stick = move · right stick left/right = turn 30° · right stick up/down = rise/sink · A or X = back to the start · trigger = play / pause.
Only what the camera saw exists in 3D. People and animals that move are rebuilt frame by frame from the side the camera saw.
